# VIP Barber — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 7, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how VIP Barber ("we", "us", "our"), operated by
VIP Infinity Technologies, T.R.Kandriga, Nagari, handles your information when
you use the VIP Barber mobile application and related services (collectively, the
"App").

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of
information as described in this policy. You can contact us any time at
**support@vipsinfinity.com**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide
- **Account details**: your name, email address, mobile number, and language preference.
- **Profile & preferences**: favourite shops, preferred barber services, and appointment preferences.
- **Booking details**: the services you book, chosen shop, date, time slots, and your queue token.
- **Shop owner / partner details**: if you register as a shop owner or partner, your shop name, address, location, timings, service menu, pricing, and gallery images.
- **Feedback & support**: messages you send through feedback forms and support tickets.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
- **Device & usage data**: app version, device type and OS, in-app actions, and analytics events.
- **Push tokens**: a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token so we can deliver booking updates and notifications to your device.
- **Crash data**: crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) used to fix bugs and improve stability.
- **Location (optional)**: with your permission, approximate location may be used to show nearby shops. You can revoke this permission any time in your device settings.

### 1.3 Payment information
- We do **not** store your card numbers or full bank details.
- When you choose **Cash**, no online payment data is collected.
- When you choose **UPI**, payment is processed through an approved third-party payment provider (such as Razorpay). Payment confirmation details (amount, status, reference) are stored on your booking. Your UPI details are handled by the payment provider under its own privacy policy and are not stored by us.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account and role (customer, shop owner, partner, or admin).
- Let you browse shops, book appointments, and manage bookings.
- Notify you about booking confirmation, reminders, queue status, and payment status.
- Send push notifications, including reminders about your scheduled appointments.
- Operate the live queue and service-timer features in participating shops.
- Store and display shop gallery images uploaded by owners.
- Process payments (cash and, where available, UPI).
- Improve the App, fix bugs, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service.
- Respond to your feedback, support requests, and legal obligations.

## 3. Legal Basis (India — DPDP Act, 2023)
We process your personal data with your consent and to perform the services you
request. You may withdraw consent or exercise your rights at any time by
contacting us at **support@vipsinfinity.com**.

## 4. Sharing & Third Parties
We share data only as needed to run the App and where permitted by law:
- **Google / Firebase** (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check): core infrastructure. See Google's Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.
- **Cloudinary**: stores and serves shop gallery images uploaded by owners.
- **Razorpay** (when enabled): processes UPI payments. See <https://razorpay.com/privacy/>.
- **Google Play / device providers**: app distribution and platform analytics.
- **Shops & owners**: booking details (name, mobile, service, time) are shared with the shop you book so they can serve you. Your email is not shared with shops.
- **Service providers**: we may engage processors who act on our instructions under confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal obligations**: we may disclose information where required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

---

## 5. Data Security
We use appropriate technical and organisational measures, including:
- Firebase security rules that restrict access to your own data and your shop's data.
- Google Cloud/Firebase encryption at rest and in transit (TLS).
- App Check with Play Integrity to protect Firebase resources.
- Signed, role-based access for admin and partner functions.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please keep your password
confidential and contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access.

---

## 6. Data Retention & Account Deletion
- We keep your data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide services, comply with law, or resolve disputes.
- You can **delete your account** directly in the App (Profile → Delete Account). This removes your account and the data we hold about you, subject to records we are legally required to keep (for example, booking and transaction records for tax/legal purposes).
- Shop owners may contact us to remove shop data and uploaded images.
